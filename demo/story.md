# The story: who is who in the zoo

You are not demoing software. You are telling a story that happens to run on software. This
file is the plot, the cast, and the tension, so you can narrate instead of describe.

**The premise:** you play yourself. For tonight, Josefina is an enterprise seller at Meridian
Cloud (a fictional provider) carrying an $8.5M target across three accounts: Nike, Patagonia,
Nordstrom. The accounts and people are invented; the seller is real.
It is Thursday. The most important meeting of your quarter is next Thursday. The week opened
with a news alert on Monday, and an hour ago a bombshell came out of your own meeting notes.

## The tension (say this out loud, in your own words)

**Beat 1, the alarm:** Monday, 8 AM. The villain cut prices a week before the meeting
that decides your quarter, and you did not go looking for that news. The brain's watchman
posted it to the team channel before you finished your coffee. Most Mondays it posts
nothing; that is why everyone reads it when it does.

**Beat 2, the habit:** your prep call with Sofia is barely over and its notes are already in
the brain's inbox. Filing happens at 6 PM every day without you; today you ran it early,
because the QBR is coming.

**Beat 3, the bomb:** the meeting notes detonate four things at once:

1. The "routine" cost review has been a live benchmark against the villain since May. You were
  wrong for two months.
2. Nobody in finance cares about egress. Priya's two months of work answered a question nobody
  asked.
3. Your champion's right hand is interviewing elsewhere.
4. The person who signs, whom you have never met, is in the room next Thursday.

**Beat 4, the mystery solved:** the hood comes off, and the graph shows the real story:
three accounts, three apparently unrelated excuses, one pattern. Customers object to the
SHAPE of the commitment, and we keep answering with discount. No single meeting contains
that sentence. Three meetings, three accounts, weeks apart, wrote that page together.

**The clock:** the news landed Monday. The meeting was today, Thursday. The QBR is next
Thursday. The demo is one week of Josefina's life, played at real speed.

**Beat 5, the turn:** intel becomes action. The brain assembles the QBR brief: who is in the
room, the NEW flag on Rachel, the contradiction not to repeat in front of Tom, the pattern
that says lead with term structure and not price. It lands as a file and in the account
team's channel, right under the morning's news alert, and only after you read it.
