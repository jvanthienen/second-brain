Subject: **Thursday prep: 10 minutes now saves you 30 in the room**

---

Hi everyone,

Thursday you are building a second brain for your accounts, live, on your own laptop. A few
minutes of prep and you spend the hour building instead of installing.

**1. Get an AI agent app working (10 min, the important one)**
Any AI that can read files on your laptop works, so use whatever you like. Claude Desktop is
the easiest and it is what I will demo with: download it, sign in (free account is fine),
ask it a question. Already on the ChatGPT desktop app? That works: use Codex inside it, not
regular chat. Terminal people: Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI and Copilot CLI all work.
One warning: the Gemini Mac app cannot read local files unless you are on Ultra, so Gemini
fans should use Gemini CLI or grab Claude Desktop for the night. Microsoft colleagues: M365
Copilot counts, and you also have internal options (Cowork, Scout): specifics in my DM.

**2. Download the workshop folder (1 min, no account needed)**
One click: https://github.com/jvanthienen/second-brain/archive/refs/heads/master.zip
No GitHub account, no git: it is a regular ZIP. Unzip it anywhere (Documents is perfect).
Do not read it all; we do that together.

**3. Introduce them to each other (2 min, the fun one)**
Open your AI, and ask it to open the folder you just unzipped (it will ask permission: say
yes). Then say: "Read AGENTS.md and BRAIN.md and tell me what this is." If it answers, you
are ahead of the class. If it fights you, no stress: we fix it together Thursday.

**4. Wire your fuel (10 min: connect first, files as plan B)**
Your brain learns from your meetings and your pipeline, so plug those in. Try the
connection first: you are on your own machine and you have the access.

- Meetings: Granola users, you are already done (your notes are local files). Fireflies or
  Otter: grab your API key from their settings. Teams: no wire exists, copy a meeting recap
  into a doc.
- Pipeline: HubSpot or Attio and you admin it? Connect the official MCP tonight. Claude
  Desktop also has a one-click Slack connector.
- Stakeholders: request your LinkedIn connections export now (LinkedIn > Settings > Data
  privacy > Get a copy of your data > tick Connections; the CSV arrives in ~10 min). This
  one is always a file, there is no wire.

Every recipe, click by click: https://www.buildergeneralist.com/second-brain

**The circuit-breaker rule:** give any connection five minutes. The moment you hit a
corporate SSO screen or an admin wall, stop: that is Friday's IT request, not tonight's
problem. Plan B always works: export files instead (transcripts as text, pipeline as CSV)
and bring 1 to 3 of them. Files are not the lesser option, they are the guaranteed one.

**No IT approvals needed.** The baseline brain uses zero connectors: no CRM login, no
calendar OAuth, nothing your security team needs to bless. Files on your laptop plus the AI
you already have.

Optional but fun: install Obsidian (https://obsidian.md, free). It draws the graph of your
brain, and it is the prettiest thing you will see on Thursday.

See you there,
Josefina
