## Who he is

Head of ML Infrastructure at [[Patagonia]]. Decides the technical stack in practice.
Does not control budget, and says so plainly.

## What we know

Consistently candid, to an unusual degree. In the August sync he disclosed the [[Vertex Cloud]]
approach unprompted, told us it came in through finance rather than through him, and then told
us exactly what would help us compete internally:

> "Give Mei a cost model she can actually defend — not a discount, a structure. And get someone
> senior from your side in front of Ana."

On commitments (2026-07-08) he was equally direct: he would personally sign one for the
certainty, but Ana won't, and the reason is optionality rather than price.

He also told us what he values most: **scheduling reliability over unit price.** He lost three
weeks waiting for hardware last year and has not forgotten it.

## How to work with him

Take what he says literally. He has twice told us precisely what to do and both times it was
correct. He is not managing us.

## Risk

He is the relationship. There is no second technical advocate at Patagonia and no executive
coverage above him — see [[Champion Dependency Risk]].
