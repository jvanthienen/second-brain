## Who she is

Head of Data at [[Relecloud Media]]. Prefers the incumbent analytics tool and is the deciding
voice on the migration.

## What we know

She is frequently described internally as a blocker. That framing is wrong and it is costing us.

At the July workshop she opened with *"I don't think we should do this, and I want to explain
why rather than just blocking it"* — then gave three specific, checkable objections:

1. Retraining eight analysts costs roughly a quarter of productivity
2. Four years of saved queries and dashboards would need rebuilding
3. The incumbent's incremental materialization has no equivalent

**The third is factually wrong** — there is an equivalent, named differently. She responded:
*"Then show me. If that's wrong, one of my three objections goes away."*

That is not a blocker. That is a serious evaluator who will change position on evidence.

Her fourth point was commercial rather than technical: they committed and are under-consuming,
so expanding scope on an unused commitment "feels backwards." See
[[Commitment Flexibility vs Discount Depth]].

## How to work with her

Bring numbers, not enthusiasm. She said explicitly that the proposal reads as though the
switching costs are zero. Costing them honestly is more persuasive than arguing they don't
matter.
