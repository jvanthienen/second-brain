## What's blocked

The [[Relecloud Media]] analytics migration — the only growth path on that account. Decision has
slipped from June to end of September.

## Why

[[Fatima Al-Rashid]] and her data team prefer the incumbent tool. She has three specific
objections and one commercial one. Critically, she is a genuine evaluator rather than a
political blocker — she said she would change position on evidence.

## Where it stands

Her four objections, as stated 2026-07-16:

| # | Objection | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retraining eight analysts ≈ a quarter of productivity | **Uncosted.** Real. |
| 2 | Four years of saved queries and dashboards to rebuild | **Uncosted.** Real. |
| 3 | No equivalent to incremental materialization | **Factually wrong** — equivalent exists, named differently |
| 4 | Under-consuming the existing commitment, so expanding "feels backwards" | Unaddressed — see [[Commitment Flexibility vs Discount Depth]] |

Two technical validation gates have passed. Objection 3 is demonstrably answerable and she has
said doing so removes it.

## Owners

- **Ours:** Priya Raman (technical), Sam Rivera (commercial)
- **Theirs:** [[Fatima Al-Rashid]] decides; Oliver Grant sponsors

## What has not been tried

- **Actually costing objections 1 and 2.** She said the proposal reads as though switching costs
  are zero, and she is right. Nobody has put numbers on them.
- Demonstrating the materialization equivalent — agreed in July, not yet done
- Addressing the under-consumption question, which she considers part of the same conversation
  and we have treated as separate
- Engaging Chris Doyle as executive sponsor

## Impact if unresolved

The account stays flat for two more years. This is the entire growth case at [[Relecloud Media]].

## Next step

Demonstrate the materialization equivalent. It is the cheapest of the four and removes a third
of her stated objections.
