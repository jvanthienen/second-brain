## The insight

Egress has surfaced in three separate conversations and is reportedly flagged by finance every
quarter, which is why it has been treated as the objection to solve.

**The evidence for that is thinner than it looks.** All three mentions trace back to
[[Daniel Osei]], and his own characterization was that it is *"the number that's easiest to
point at"* rather than a real problem. [[Sofia Marchetti]] pushed to take it to finance to kill
it as a topic — which is not the same as finance caring about it.

We have never heard it from anyone in finance directly.
