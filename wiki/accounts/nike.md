## Current state

Consumption is up 18% year over year and drawdown is running ahead of the commitment curve. The
technical story is the strongest it has been: the consumer-data analytics platform migration went live in June
and cut on-call volume by roughly a third, which removed the account's longest-standing
complaint. [[Daniel Osei]] is now willing to say so publicly, which he wasn't three months ago.

The renewal opened unusually early — [[Tom Bradley]] in procurement raised it nine months ahead,
citing a preference for long runway on large agreements. Running alongside it is a finance-led
cost-optimization review. Tom described this in June as a routine internal exercise not aimed at
us, and we have treated it as low risk since.

The commercial relationship remains concentrated in engineering. [[Sofia Marchetti]] is a
genuine advocate and has offered to brief us before the CFO meeting.

---

## Commercial

Renewal is the whole game. Current agreement ends March 2027, tracking ahead of pace, no
disputes. The open question is not whether they renew but on what terms — and we have never
discussed terms with anyone who sets them.

Our working assumption has been that **egress cost is the main commercial objection.** It has
surfaced in three separate conversations and finance reportedly flags it every quarter.
[[Egress Cost Proposal]] is the response, due 2026-08-20.

## Technical

The consumer-data analytics platform migration completed 2026-06-19 and is the reference point
for the relationship.
Twelve services cut over with no rollbacks.

Remaining technical friction is egress cost, which is architectural rather than pricing —
regional caching is the main lever.

## Risks

  timing is uncomfortable.
- **No relationship with the economic buyer** with eight months to signature.
- **Board-level cost transformation** has put every multi-year obligation under review, which

  advocate.
