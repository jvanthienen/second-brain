# Agendas & meeting invites

> Voice rules come from [`../docs/soul.md`](../docs/soul.md) — this file only covers structure.

**Default is short.** Goal + numbered agenda. Nothing else unless asked.

---

## Standard agenda

```
## [Meeting name] — [Party A] × [Party B]

**Attendees:** [Name (Org/Role)] · [Name] · [duration] · [scope guard, if needed]

**Goal**
[1–2 sentences. What must be true when we hang up. One goal, not three.]

**Agenda**
1. [Item] *(N min)*
2. [Item] *(N min)*
3. [Item] *(N min)*
```

### Rules

- **One goal.** If there are two, it's two meetings — or one of them is the real one. Say which.
- **State the goal as an outcome, not a topic.** "Agree a dated milestone and a named owner," not
  "discuss the integration."
- **Time-box every item, and make them sum to the meeting length.** An agenda that overruns on
  paper will overrun in the room.
- **3–5 items.** More than five in 30 minutes is a wish list.
- **Name a lead per item only** when three or more organisations are in the room. Otherwise it
  reads as bureaucracy.
- **Scope guard** — add to the header when the meeting risks sprawl: `**Scope: X only**`. Quote
  the person who set the boundary if there is one; it holds better than a rule.
- **No pre-reading dump.** Links go at the bottom, or in a separate mail.
- **Say what is *not* on the agenda** only when someone is likely to raise it. One line.

---

## When to go long

Add these **only** for escalations, exec alignment, or a meeting whose failure mode is known.
Never by default.

- **Exit criteria** — "Three things that must be true when we hang up." Use when the meeting
  could end in agreement that commits no one.
- **Facilitator notes** — what not to raise, sensitive framing, the fallback argument. For your
  eyes only, **never in the invite body**.
- **Watch for** — the specific failure mode. One line.

---

## Invite body

Shorter than the agenda document. Goal, agenda, logistics. **No facilitator notes — attendees
see this.**

```
Goal: [one sentence]

1. [Item] (N min)
2. [Item] (N min)
3. [Item] (N min)

[Join link / location]
```

- Subject: `[Party A] × [Party B] — [Topic]`. No "Sync," "Touchbase" or "Quick chat."
- If the meeting has a scope boundary, put it in the first line of the body, not the subject.
- **State the time zone on every time.** Add the attendee's local time when they're outside
  yours.

---

## Anti-patterns

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| "Discuss migration status" | "Agree a re-scoped plan with a named owner and a dated milestone" |
| Five agenda items, no times | Three items, time-boxed, summing to 30 min |
| Goal that restates the meeting title | Goal that states what changes because the meeting happened |
| Facilitator notes pasted into the invite | Facilitator notes kept in the prep doc |
| "Sync on Nike" | "Nike × Us — renewal structure working session" |
