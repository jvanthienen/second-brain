# Playbook — Meeting agenda & invite

Read [`docs/voice.md`](../docs/voice.md) first.

---

## Structure

```
Title: [Account] — [specific purpose]

PURPOSE
[One sentence. What this meeting is for.]

OUTCOME
[What's different when it ends. A decision, a plan, a commitment.]

AGENDA
  5   Context — where we are
 20   [Main topic]
 20   [Main topic]
 10   Next steps and owners

PRE-READ
[Link, or "none".]

ATTENDEES
[Name — role — why they're here]
```

---

## Rules

- **Titles name the purpose.** "Northwind — renewal structure" not "Northwind sync".
- **Every meeting has a stated outcome.** If you can't write one, cancel it.
- **Minutes against each item.** Forces realism and stops the first topic eating the hour.
- **Justify every attendee.** If you can't say why someone is there, they aren't.
- **Pre-reads go out 24 hours ahead**, or don't exist. A pre-read sent an hour before is a
  document nobody read.
- **Last 10 minutes are always next steps.** Owners and dates, captured live.

---

## Example

```
Title: Northwind — renewal structure and cost review

PURPOSE
Align on the renewal shape before Northwind's September finance review.

OUTCOME
Agreement on commitment structure, and a written answer on egress costs.

AGENDA
  5   Where we are — consumption vs commitment curve
 20   Renewal structure options
 20   Egress cost proposal
 10   Next steps and owners

PRE-READ
Two-page renewal outline (sent 24h ahead)

ATTENDEES
Rachel Kim    CFO, Northwind        — signs the renewal
Sofia Marchetti  VP Eng, Northwind  — technical sponsor
Sam Rivera    Account Executive     — owns the commercial
Priya Raman   Solution Engineer     — owns the egress proposal
```
