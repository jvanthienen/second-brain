# Playbook — Email

Read [`docs/soul.md`](../docs/soul.md) first. **Default to the short version.**

---

## Structure

```
Subject: [what it's about — specific, no "Follow-up" or "Touching base"]

[Line 1: what changed or why you're writing. The news.]

[Body: 2–4 sentences max, or 3–5 bullets. One idea per bullet.]

[The ask: who, what, by when.]

[Sign-off]
```

**Length:** under 150 words. If it needs more, it needs a meeting or a document.

---

## Rules

- **Subject lines carry information.** "Northwind renewal — CFO joining the QBR" not "Quick
  update".
- **No preamble.** Delete "I hope this finds you well" and "I wanted to reach out".
- **One ask per email.** Two asks means one gets ignored — and you don't choose which.
- **Dates, not adverbs.** "By Thursday the 14th" not "soon" or "at your earliest convenience".
- **Never send automatically.** Draft, review, send.

---

## Example — customer, exec

```
Subject: Northwind renewal — proposal for the September review

Rachel,

Following the QBR, here's the shape of what we'd propose for the renewal.

The current agreement runs to March. Consumption is tracking about 6% ahead of
the commitment curve, so the renewal question is really about predictability
rather than volume.

I've attached a two-page outline covering the commitment structure and how we'd
handle the egress costs Daniel raised.

Could we take 30 minutes before your September review? I'm flexible the week of
the 14th.

Sam
```

## Example — internal, ask

```
Subject: Tailspin — need an exec sponsor

Dana,

A competitor is targeting Tailspin's training workload with aggressive GPU
pricing. Our champion confirmed it on the 4th and prefers to stay with us, but
their CTO has never met anyone from our side above me.

That gap is now being used against us.

Can you introduce Ana Petrov (CTO) to someone at VP level this quarter? I'd
suggest tying it to the inference-optimization work — it gives the meeting a
reason to exist beyond relationship-building.

Sam
```
