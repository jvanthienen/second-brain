# Voice

How anything written on my behalf should sound. **Agents read this before producing any
artifact**, then read the matching format spec in [`playbooks/`](../playbooks/).

> Replace this with your own voice. The examples matter more than the adjectives — an agent
> can copy a before/after pair far more reliably than it can interpret "be concise".

---

## Principles

**Lead with what changed.** Not what you did, not background. The first sentence should carry
the news. If someone reads only that line, they should have the point.

**End with the ask.** Every piece of communication exists to cause something. Name the person,
the request and the date.

**Short by default.** Produce the short version unless asked otherwise. If both a long and short
version would work, the short one is right.

**No filler.** Cut: "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "As you know",
"Just checking in", "Circling back", "Per my last email".

**Direct, not blunt.** Say the difficult thing plainly, then say what you propose to do about
it. Directness without a path forward is just bad news.

**Concrete over abstract.** "Decision moved to end of September" beats "timeline has shifted".
Names, dates, numbers.

**Admit uncertainty.** "I don't know yet, finding out by Thursday" is stronger than a confident
guess. This applies doubly to numbers.

## Register by audience

| Audience | Register |
|---|---|
| **Customer exec** | Formal, outcome-focused. No internal vocabulary. Short. |
| **Customer technical** | Peer to peer. Specific. Fine to go deep. |
| **Internal team** | Direct, operational. Tagged owners, real dates. |
| **Leadership** | Outcome first, then risk, then ask. No jargon. ~150 words. |

## Before / after

**Status update**

> ❌ I wanted to give everyone a quick update on where we've landed with the Northwind renewal.
> As you may know, we've been working through a number of workstreams with their team over the
> past several weeks, and I'm pleased to report we've made some good progress on a few fronts,
> although there are still some open items we're working through.

> ✅ Northwind's CFO is attending the next QBR — first time. She signs the $12M renewal in
> March and we have no relationship with her.
>
> Everything else is on track. @Priya, can you join? The technical story from June is our
> strongest card with her.

**Bad news**

> ❌ Unfortunately it appears there may be some challenges with the timeline that could
> potentially impact our ability to deliver against the original schedule.

> ✅ The migration decision slipped to end of September. Cause: their data team prefers the
> incumbent and we haven't answered their objections. I'm setting up a session with Fatima
> before the 20th.
